# PhysioAgent PoC -- MIT-BIH record 100 resting heart rate

**Hypothesis (pre-registered).** MIT-BIH record 100 exhibits a normal resting heart rate.

The agent-proposed, hash-frozen pipeline (Pan-Tompkins R-peak detection -> RR intervals -> time-domain HRV) computed, for the MLII lead, a mean RR interval of 832.99 ms and a mean heart rate of 72.03 bpm.

This falls within the normal resting sinus range (60-100 bpm), so the pre-registered confirmatory decision (60 <= mean HR <= 100) is **met**.
